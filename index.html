Dynamic Voice Engine

Zero Confusion. Absolute Flow.

The vertical timeline keeps everyone focused. Current spoken lines are magnified and auto-scrolled in sync with studio audio. If the door rings or a player asks a question, pause or rewind with a single tap.

  • ✓
    Dual-Source Audio:

    Pristine human voice recordings with automatic fallback to high-quality neural speech synthesizers.

  • ✓
    Screen Keep-Awake:

    Prevents iOS from sleeping mid-turn, so the host can glance at the timer and status anytime.

  • ✓
    Tactile Haptics:

    Discreet sensory vibration alerts notify the host when night steps begin or timers expire.
