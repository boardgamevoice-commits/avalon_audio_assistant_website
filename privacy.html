Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · App Version 1.2.5

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Board Game Audio Assistant ("we", "our", or "the application") handles information when you use our iOS, iPadOS, and iPhone Duo application. We design our software offline-first with minimal data retention and strict local sandboxing.

Summary: Zero account registration, zero cloud user profiles, and zero tracking of private conversations. Custom scripts, voice recordings, local Wi-Fi rooms, and match scoreboards remain strictly on your device.

2. On-Device Sandbox & Permissions

  • Local App Preferences & Sandbox Storage: Voice settings (language, volume, speech rate), custom game configs, and scoreboard histories are stored locally in Apple's app sandbox and UserDefaults, with cache/rebuildable directories marked isExcludedFromBackup = true.
  • Microphone Permission (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription, Optional): Requested only when you enter the Voice Studio to record custom host lines. Recordings stay 100% inside your device's local sandbox and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Local Wi-Fi Room (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription, Optional): Requested only when hosting or joining a nearby game room over the same Wi-Fi or personal hotspot via Bonjour (_avalon-room._tcp). Secret role dealing and mission votes travel strictly peer-to-peer between local devices without touching cloud servers.
  • No Personal Identity Collection: We never ask for or collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, GPS coordinates, or payment card details.

3. StoreKit 2 In-App Purchases & Ad-Free PRO

Optional one-time In-App Purchases (such as Lifetime Ad-Free PRO, com.avalon.lifetime_pro) and Restore Purchases are processed directly and exclusively by Apple via native StoreKit 2. We never receive or store your billing address or payment credentials. Once Lifetime PRO is unlocked (including via Apple Family Sharing), ad requests are permanently disabled on your device.

4. Advertising, UMP Consent & ATT (Google AdMob)

The free tier of Board Game Audio Assistant utilizes Google AdMob (and Google User Messaging Platform, UMP, for GDPR/regional privacy consent) to display banner, interstitial, and optional 24-hour rewarded ad-free pass advertisements.

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT / NSUserTrackingUsageDescription): On iOS 14.5+, we request explicit permission before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you select "Ask App Not to Track", AdMob serves non-personalized contextual ads only.
  • Authorized Digital Sellers: Verified via /app-ads.txt (Publisher ID: pub-2034595640300550).
  • Learn more at Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

5. Speech Synthesis & Children's Privacy

Pre-recorded studio narration plays 100% offline from the app bundle, with fallback to Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under 13.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月 · 同步版本 v1.2.5

1. 核心承诺

桌游语音法官（Board Game Audio Assistant）坚持离线优先与最小化数据原则。应用无需注册账号，不建立云端用户画像，所有自定义板子、录音音频、局域网房间与记分历史均 100% 保存在您的设备本地沙盒中。

2. 本地沙盒存储与系统权限说明

  • 本地偏好与备份隔离：语言、语速、音量及历史记分保存在本地 UserDefaults 与应用沙盒中，可重建缓存目录声明 isExcludedFromBackup = true。
  • 麦克风权限（可选）：仅在您主动进入“录音工坊”录制自定义主持语音时请求，录音文件严格保存在本机沙盒，绝不上传至任何服务器。
  • 本地局域网权限（可选）：仅在您主动创建或加入面对面同 Wi-Fi 房间（Bonjour _avalon-room._tcp）时请求，秘密发牌与投票数据仅在局域网设备间点对点传输，不经过外网云端。

3. StoreKit 2 应用内购买与免广告权益

应用提供可选的一次性买断内购（终身免广告专业版，com.avalon.lifetime_pro，支持 Apple 家人共享与一键恢复购买）。所有支付均由 Apple 官方 StoreKit 2 安全处理，开发者不会接触或存储任何支付账户信息。解锁专业版后，应用将在本地永久关闭广告请求。

4. 广告服务、UMP 同意框架与 ATT 追踪透明

免费版集成 Google AdMob（含 Google UMP 隐私同意弹窗与可选 24 小时激励视频免广告通行证）。在 iOS 14.5+ 设备上，应用遵循 Apple App Tracking Transparency（ATT）规范征求授权；若您选择“要求应用不跟踪”，仅展示非个性化上下文广告。授权卖家记录详见 /app-ads.txt（pub-2034595640300550）。
